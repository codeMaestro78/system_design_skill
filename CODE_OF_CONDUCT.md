# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a professional exchange. We pledge to keep discussions focused on the work, to assume good faith, and to accept that a disagreement about a design is a disagreement about the design, not about the person holding it.

## Expected behavior

- Review the change, not the contributor.
- Explain what a change does and why it is needed.
- Quote evidence from the repository when disputing a claim.
- Accept review feedback, and push back with a reason or an alternative.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or insulting comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissal of other people's work.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, evaluations, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening an issue that asks the maintainer (@codeMaestro78) for a private contact method, or by contacting the maintainer directly through GitHub. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content, comment, or restrict participation as the situation requires, and may warn, warn and restrict, or ban a contributor permanently.
